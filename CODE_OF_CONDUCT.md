# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. This project deals with destructive filesystem operations,
so disagreement about safety is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing and publishing another person's private paths or credentials are
not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate these rules.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and private project communication.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban
participants who violate it.

Report conduct concerns privately to **evilleader91@gmail.com**.
